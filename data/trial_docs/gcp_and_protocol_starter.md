## Demo trial TRL-METAB-07 — Inclusion/Exclusion summary (synthetic, for this capstone only)
- Inclusion: Age 18-75; Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus diagnosis >= 6 months; eGFR >= 30 mL/min/1.73m2; HbA1c 7.0-10.0%.
- Exclusion: Pregnant or breastfeeding; currently on an investigational agent or stopped one less than 30 days ago (washout);
  history of a malignancy EXCEPT if in documented remission for more than 2 years and the malignancy was non-recurring
  (site PI makes the final call on borderline remission-window cases — not a pure protocol-text determination).
- Any informed consent procedure performed before the ICF is signed and dated is a protocol deviation and must be
  reported to the PI and IRB per ICH-GCP 4.8 and 4.9.

## ICH-GCP E6(R2) — key sections relevant to pre-screening
- **4.8 Informed Consent**: Freely given, written, signed and personally dated by the subject (or legally acceptable
  representative) and by the person conducting the consent discussion, BEFORE any trial-related procedure is carried out.
- **4.9 Records and Reports**: Source documents must support consent timing, eligibility assessment, and any deviation.
- **3. IRB/IEC**: No subject may be screened under a protocol version the IRB/IEC has not approved. The site must be
  screening under the current, approved consent version — an undated or missing IRB reference is a documentation gap
  that should be resolved before enrollment, even if the underlying eligibility looks fine.
- **5.5 Prior malignancy / remission judgment calls**: Where a protocol allows an exception for malignancy in remission
  beyond a stated window, borderline cases (e.g. remission duration close to the cutoff, ambiguous recurrence history)
  are a clinical judgment for the Principal Investigator, not a pass/fail that should be computed mechanically.
